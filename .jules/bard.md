## 2026-05-15 - [Visibility in Workspace Refactoring]
**Confusion:** Refactoring crate-internal utilities and parsers to `pub(crate)` across a workspace can accidentally break downstream binaries (like `duke/src/jar_diff.rs`) if they depend on internal structure (e.g. `duke_classfile::types` re-exports or `Option<&Option<CpEntry>>` type resolution).
**Clarification:** When narrowing visibility to satisfy documentation and architecture requirements, always run a full workspace compilation (`cargo clippy --all-targets --all-features`) to ensure binary crates or tests that relied on the previously public internals are updated or that the narrowing is rolled back if the items must remain public.
