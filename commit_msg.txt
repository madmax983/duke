⚡ Bolt: [performance improvement] Pre-allocate arrays and remove intermediate Vec allocations

Removed intermediate `Vec::new()` and `push()` patterns in favor of direct iterator and index mapping to reduce redundant heap allocations.

- `duke/src/main.rs`: Removed intermediate `Vec<Slot>` construction for `string_args`. Now initializes the final `[Ljava/lang/String;` heap allocation and populates it directly.
- `duke/src/jar_diff.rs`: Optimized set `.difference()` mappings using `.copied().cloned().collect()` chaining.
