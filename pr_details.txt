Title: 🌟 Nova: Static Bytecode Smells Analyzer

Description:
💡 **The Spark:** I noticed we decode and verify bytecode structure, but we don't look for logical inefficiencies or anti-patterns generated by compilers (or obfuscators).
🚀 **The Feature:** Implemented a new `analyzer.rs` module in `duke-bytecode` with a `BytecodeSmell` enum and `analyze_smells` function. It statically detects patterns like `PushImmediatePop` (pushing a value and immediately popping it), `InfiniteLoop` (gotos pointing to themselves), and `RedundantGoto` (gotos pointing to the immediate next instruction).
🔮 **The Potential:** This lays the groundwork for a future `--lint` mode in the CLI, where Duke could optionally warn developers about poorly optimized classes, or it could be used by a JIT/AOT compiler phase to perform peephole optimizations.
⚠️ **Risk:** Extremely low. Completely isolated module, pure function, and hidden behind the `nova` feature flag so it doesn't add overhead to the default build.
