Title: 🗺️ Atlas: Encapsulate leaky abstract public modules
Body:
🕸️ Tangle: The `duke_classfile` API exported an intermediate `types` module (`pub mod signature`) merely to re-export its inner types, and `duke-telemetry` exposed internal serialization helpers via `pub mod ser_helpers`. This creates confusing, leaky abstractions and redundant paths.

📐 Blueprint: Encapsulated both modules by changing visibility to `pub(crate)`.

🧱 Stability: Reduced coupling, removed redundant namespace paths.

🔬 Verification: Builds successfully, strict separation enforced. `cargo doc` warnings fixed.
